AZOBSS PATCH 695

Admin Payment Alerts now show the specific JUPEM document category instead of the generic label.

Examples:
- JUPEM Document Purchase PA (1 unit)
- JUPEM Document Purchase BM (2 unit)
- JUPEM Document Purchase Lot Kadaster Berdigit (1 unit)
- Mixed cart: JUPEM Document Purchase PA (1 unit) + BM (1 unit)

Covered categories:
PA, BM, SBM, GPS, Syit Piawai, and Lot Kadaster Berdigit (NDCDB / NDCDB_C3).

The change is applied in both deploy-server.js and backend/server.js. Public PA product names such as Pelan Akui PA123 are not overwritten.

Existing generic PA/BM alerts are also backfilled automatically from premiumOrders when Payment Alerts is loaded/refreshed.

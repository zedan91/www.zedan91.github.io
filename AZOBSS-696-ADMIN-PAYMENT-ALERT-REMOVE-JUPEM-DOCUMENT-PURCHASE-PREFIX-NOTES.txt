AZOBSS PATCH 696

The phrase "JUPEM Document Purchase" has been removed from PA/BM payment product names and admin Payment Alerts.

Examples:
- PA (1 unit)
- BM (2 unit)
- Lot Kadaster Berdigit (1 unit)
- Mixed cart: PA (1 unit) + BM (1 unit)

Covered categories:
PA, BM, SBM, GPS, Syit Piawai, and Lot Kadaster Berdigit (NDCDB / NDCDB_C3).

The change is applied in both deploy-server.js and backend/server.js. Existing PA/BM alerts are backfilled automatically from premiumOrders when Payment Alerts is loaded or refreshed.

AZOBSS PATCH 765
Date: 2026-08-04

1. Paid receipt delivery marker
- Every Paid row now shows RECEIPT NOT SENT until it is marked/shared.
- Native/WhatsApp PDF sharing marks the receipt as SENT after the share action succeeds.
- A circular envelope action lets the admin manually toggle SENT / NOT SENT.
- The state is stored in Firestore so it survives refresh and can also cover website sales.

2. Manual Invoice ToyyibPay total synchronization
- Root cause fixed: an older ToyyibPay Bill Code could remain at RM250 after another RM20 item was added and the invoice total became RM270.
- Backend now recalculates the payable amount from all invoice items, discount and shipping charged.
- Pending legacy bills are regenerated once under amount-sync version 765.
- The exact ToyyibPay bill amount and version are stored on the invoice and payment order.
- Frontend refuses to reuse a cached Bill Code/QR unless its saved amount matches the current invoice total.
- Paid synchronization also uses the recalculated invoice total, preventing a stale gross value from producing the wrong receipt total.

Deployment
- Upload all files.
- Restart / Manual Deploy the Render backend because deploy-server.js changed.
- Press Ctrl+F5 in the Admin page.
- Generate/download the Pending invoice PDF again. A new Bill Code and QR will be produced for RM270.00.
- Do not send/use the older RM250 PDF/QR.

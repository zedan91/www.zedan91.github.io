AZOBSS Patch 688

Fix:
- When the cursor hovers over a service card and its hidden details expand, neighbouring cards no longer stretch to the same height.
- The homepage service grid now aligns each card to the top with its own independent height.
- Brownies card position, image, link and other website functions are preserved.

Cause:
A late global CSS rule changed .services-grid to flex and forced align-items: stretch, so every card in the same flex row followed the tallest hovered card.

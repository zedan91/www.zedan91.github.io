AZOBSS PATCH 730 - ADMIN INVOICE + RECEIPT DOCUMENTS

Baseline: Patch 729.

Changes:
1. Receipt table now uses No., Description, Category, Qty, Unit Price and Amount.
2. Header labels and item content are vertically centered inside table cells.
3. Description text is centered and wraps safely for long product/service names.
4. Separate Receipt and Invoice menus are available for every sale record.
5. Both documents support Download PDF, WhatsApp, Telegram and Print.
6. Invoice uses a derived invoice number (AZR -> AZI, otherwise INV- prefix), Bill To, Issue Date, Payment Terms and Total Payable.
7. Receipt remains payment-focused with Payment Method, status and Total Paid when status is Paid.
8. Invoice footer clearly states that an invoice is a payment request and not proof of payment.
9. Ink-saving grey design, AZOBSS logo and existing sales calculations are preserved.

No new Render environment variables, Cloudflare Worker changes or Firebase Rules are required.

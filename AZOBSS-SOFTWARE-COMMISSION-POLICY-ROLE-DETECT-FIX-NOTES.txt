AZOBSS PATCH 403 - Software Commission Policy Role Detect Fix

Issue:
- Software Tools showed only "Private Sales Access" under Commission Policy.
- Staff/semiAdmin percentage rows were hidden because role detection was not ready when the page rendered.

Fix:
- Software Tools now detects role from multiple sources:
  - azCurrentSoftwareOwner()
  - localStorage/sessionStorage profile objects
  - direct role cache keys
  - staff/semi-admin mode fallback
- Commission policy re-renders multiple times after page load/auth/profile events.
- If role is staff: show Staff 70% Commission and AZOBSS 30%.
- If role is semiAdmin: show Semi-admin 90% Commission and AZOBSS 10%.
- Private Sales Access remains visible below the role-specific policy.

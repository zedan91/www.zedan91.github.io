AZOBSS Patch 832 — WhatsApp Ultra Fast Open + Auto Close

- Baseline: 831.
- Frontend starts the booking POST immediately with keepalive.
- WhatsApp app launch fallback reduced from 2.5 s to about 250 ms.
- Protocol navigation delay reduced from 120 ms to about 25 ms.
- Launcher auto-close after external WhatsApp takes focus reduced from ~8 s blur fallback to ~450 ms.
- visibility hidden closes in ~180 ms; pagehide closes in ~120 ms.
- Browser security dialog Open WhatsApp? cannot be auto-clicked.
- A website cannot detect the actual WhatsApp Send button/message-delivery event. The launcher closes when the app takes focus, normally before the user sends.
- Background booking save, keepalive and duplicate protection remain unchanged.

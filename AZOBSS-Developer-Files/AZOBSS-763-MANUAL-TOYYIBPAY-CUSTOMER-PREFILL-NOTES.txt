AZOBSS PATCH 763 - MANUAL TOYYIBPAY CUSTOMER PREFILL
Date: 2026-08-04
Baseline: (762)

Changes:
- Manual Pending ToyyibPay bills now always use billPayorInfo=1.
- billTo, billPhone and billEmail are sent from the manual invoice customer fields.
- Customer name and phone are prefilled on the ToyyibPay payment page.
- Email remains blank/editable when the invoice has no email, because ToyyibPay requires the payer to enter it.
- Existing Pending bills created before patch 763 are superseded and regenerated once so their new payment page contains the payer prefill.
- Changing customer name, phone, email or invoice amount regenerates the bill with current details.
- RM1 automatic ToyyibPay payment fee from patch 762 is preserved.

Deployment:
- Upload the full ZIP contents.
- Restart / Manual Deploy the Render backend because deploy-server.js changed.
- Generate/download the invoice PDF again; the regenerated QR uses the new Bill Code.

AZOBSS PATCH 767 — MANUAL TOYYIBPAY OPTIONAL EMAIL / OPEN BILL
Date: 2026-08-04

Changes
- Manual invoice Email is optional again and the email field aligns with Customer Name and Phone.
- If customer name, phone and a valid email are all available, ToyyibPay uses billPayorInfo=1 and prefills all three fields.
- If any payer detail is missing, ToyyibPay uses billPayorInfo=0 and billTo/billPhone/billEmail are intentionally blank, so the customer completes all mandatory payer fields on ToyyibPay.
- A non-empty but invalid email is rejected by the admin form; a blank email is accepted.
- The payer mode is stored on the invoice/order and a pending bill is recreated only when the payer mode actually changes.
- All fixes from patches 762-766 remain included, including RM1 payment fee, RM270 amount sync, receipt-sent marker, customer prefill when complete, and left-aligned PDF descriptions.

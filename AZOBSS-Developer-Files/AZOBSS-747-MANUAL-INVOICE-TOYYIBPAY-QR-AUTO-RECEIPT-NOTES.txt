AZOBSS PATCH 747 — MANUAL INVOICE TOYYIBPAY QR + AUTO PAID RECEIPT
Date: 2026-08-04
Baseline: Patch 746

WHAT CHANGED
1. Manual Sales & Receipts invoice with Status = Pending and Payment Method = ToyyibPay creates a ToyyibPay bill through the existing backend integration.
2. Invoice PDF includes a scannable ToyyibPay payment QR, amount and Bill Code.
3. A Pay icon appears on eligible pending manual invoices.
4. ToyyibPay callback/API verification updates the SAME Firestore receipts document:
   - Pending Invoice (AZI-...) -> Paid Receipt (AZR-...)
   - paid gross, recognized costs and net profit become active
   - payment reference, Bill Code and verified time are recorded
5. The active Sales & Receipts page listens for changes to manual receipts and refreshes automatically after payment confirmation.
6. Manual invoice payment orders are excluded from website-order rows to prevent duplicate ledger entries.
7. If the invoice amount changes after a bill was created, a new bill/order reference is generated. A stale old bill is not allowed to convert the latest invoice.

RENDER / BACKEND
- deploy-server.js changed, so Render must redeploy.
- package.json now includes qrcode 1.5.4. Render will install it during deployment.
- Existing ToyyibPay variables are reused. No new required secret is introduced.
- Optional expiry setting:
  AZOBSS_MANUAL_INVOICE_EXPIRY_DAYS=7

HOW TO USE
1. Create a manual record.
2. Set Status = Pending.
3. Set Payment Method = ToyyibPay.
4. Enter a total above RM0 and save.
5. Download/share/print the Invoice. The QR appears in the PDF.
6. Customer scans and pays.
7. After ToyyibPay verification, the same record changes to Paid Receipt automatically.

NO CHANGE REQUIRED
- Cloudflare Worker
- Firebase Rules

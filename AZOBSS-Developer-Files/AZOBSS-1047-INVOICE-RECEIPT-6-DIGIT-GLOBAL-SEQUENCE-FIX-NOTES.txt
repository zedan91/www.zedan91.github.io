AZOBSS v1047 - Invoice / Receipt 6-Digit Global Running Sequence

Scope
- Admin > Sales & Receipts manual invoice / receipt numbering.

New format
- Invoice: AZI-YYYYMMDD-000001
- Receipt: AZR-YYYYMMDD-000001

Behaviour
1. The YYYYMMDD section remains so the document date is visible directly in the number.
2. The final numeric sequence is now 6 digits.
3. The sequence no longer resets every day. It continues globally across dates.
4. Example: AZI-20260828-000047 -> next new document on 29 Aug becomes AZI-20260829-000048.
5. A paid receipt derived from an invoice keeps the same date/sequence suffix and changes AZI -> AZR, preserving invoice/receipt pairing.
6. Existing legacy 4-digit numbers remain readable and are not automatically renumbered merely by opening/editing the same document.
7. Duplicate-number repair now allocates replacement numbers from the global maximum sequence and writes 6 digits.

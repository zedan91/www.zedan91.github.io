AZOBSS Patch 759 - Firestore Quota Saver

- Removed 15-second full Sales & Receipts polling.
- Paid-status refresh now runs at most once every 5 minutes and only when a Pending manual invoice exists.
- Reuses an invoice's stored ToyyibPay bill, payment URL and QR without another backend/Firestore call.
- Adds 60-second frontend load cache and reduces list queries to 300 rows.
- Uses fast trusted-admin token verification for temporary PDF endpoints without a Firestore user-profile lookup.
- Stops writing an admin audit record for every ephemeral temporary PDF/ZIP upload.
- Keeps cached rows visible when Firestore returns RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED.

Existing exhausted Firebase quota may need time to reset. Existing invoices with a stored QR can still generate PDFs without another bill lookup.

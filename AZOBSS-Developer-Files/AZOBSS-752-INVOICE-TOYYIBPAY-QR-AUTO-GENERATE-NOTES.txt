AZOBSS Patch 752

- Every manual Pending invoice now automatically uses ToyyibPay.
- The Invoice PDF preparation step creates/reuses the unique ToyyibPay Bill Code and embeds its QR.
- Older Pending manual invoices also receive a QR the next time Invoice Download, Share, Print, Copy Link, or Open Payment is used.
- The payment-method selector is locked to ToyyibPay while status is Pending.
- Paid/Refunded/Cancelled records keep their normal editable payment method.
- After ToyyibPay callback verification, the same Firestore record changes from Pending Invoice to Paid Receipt.

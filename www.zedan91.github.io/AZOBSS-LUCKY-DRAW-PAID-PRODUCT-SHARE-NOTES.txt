Patch 125 - Lucky Draw Paid Product Share Requirement

Scope:
- Lucky Draw only + paid Software/CAD product share button.

Changes:
- Join Lucky Draw now requires at least 1 valid click from a paid Software/CAD product share link for the current month.
- Added "Share for Lucky Draw" button to paid Software Tools and paid CAD Tools cards.
- Product share link format:
  /Software-Tools/?p=<productId>&r=<username>
  /CAD-Tools-&-Resources/?p=<productId>&r=<username>
- Visitor opening the paid product share link records a valid product share click.
- Self-click and duplicate device/IP clicks are not counted.
- Lucky Draw user status/check now uses product share click status, not normal Lucky Draw invite link.
- Backend join endpoint blocks join if product share click count is 0.

No Firebase Rules update required.

AZOBSS Patch 736 — Sales & Receipts automatic load race fix
Date: 2026-08-04

Fixes a timing issue where the Sales & Receipts tab could become active before its ES module finished importing Firebase. In that state the optional loader call was skipped, leaving the table at 'Open Sales & Receipts to load records.' with zero KPI cards.

Changes:
- Admin tab waits up to 10 seconds for window.azSalesReceiptsLoad to become available.
- Sales & Receipts module auto-loads when the section is already active.
- Auto-load retries when Firebase Auth becomes ready.
- MutationObserver loads records whenever the section receives the active class.
- Module query version bumped to v=736 to bypass browser cache.

No Cloudflare Worker, Render environment variable, backend route, or Firebase Rules change is required.

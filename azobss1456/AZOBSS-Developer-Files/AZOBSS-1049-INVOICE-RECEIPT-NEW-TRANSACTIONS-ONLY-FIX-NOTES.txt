AZOBSS PATCH 1049
=================

Purpose:
Keep historical automatic Invoice / Receipt records unchanged. Apply the 6-digit AZI/AZR scheme only to new automatic transactions after this patch is deployed.

Rules:
- No historical backfill.
- Admin load does not write/renumber old records.
- Opening an old receipt does not allocate a new number.
- Existing old premiumOrders remain with legacy IDs.
- New premiumOrders receive AZI at first persist.
- Paid newly-numbered order receives matching AZR sequence.
- Internal payment references remain untouched.

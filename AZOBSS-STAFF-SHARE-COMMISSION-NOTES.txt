AZOBSS Staff Share Commission Patch (171)

Purpose:
- Adds commission tracking for staff/semi-admin who share paid Software/CAD product links.
- Existing owner commission remains:
  * Staff owner sells own product: owner 70% / AZOBSS 30%.
- New share-link policy:
  * Staff shares admin/AZOBSS product: sharer 20% / AZOBSS 80%.
  * Staff shares another staff-owned product: owner 60% / sharer 10% / AZOBSS 30%.
  * Owner shares own product: no double commission; owner remains 70%.

Files changed:
- Software-Tools/index.html
- CAD-Tools-&-Resources/index.html
- backend/server.js

How it works:
- Product share links keep using ?p=PRODUCT_ID&r=username.
- When a buyer opens a shared link, the ref is stored locally for that product.
- When payment bill is created, Software/CAD sends staffReferral + product owner metadata to backend.
- When payment is verified/paid, backend creates commissionRecords.

Important Render requirement for automatic Firestore commission:
- Add Firebase service account env to azobss-backend if commissionRecords should be written directly to Firestore:
  FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON={service account JSON}
- If this env is not set, backend will fallback to backend/data/commission-records.json only.

Firestore Rules:
- No new Firestore Rules are required when backend writes via Firebase Admin SDK.

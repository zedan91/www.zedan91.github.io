AZOBSS PATCH 731 - INVOICE TO RECEIPT STATUS CONVERSION
Date: 2026-08-04
Baseline: Patch 730

Changes
=======
1. Status now controls the active customer document:
   - Pending / Cancelled / Failed = Invoice
   - Paid / Refunded = Receipt
2. A Pending manual record uses an AZI invoice number.
3. When status changes from Pending to Paid, the same record converts to an AZR receipt number while retaining the invoice reference.
4. Pending invoices are not recognized in Paid Gross Sales, Total Costs, Net Profit, Physical Sales or Software / Digital totals.
5. Pending table rows show RM0.00 as recognized gross/cost/profit and show the actual invoice amount as Amount Due.
6. After changing to Paid and saving, gross, costs and net profit are immediately recognized and dashboard KPIs refresh.
7. Only the relevant document menu is shown:
   - Pending: Invoice actions
   - Paid: Receipt actions
8. The manual form title, document-number label, item heading, calculation labels and Save button change automatically with status.
9. PDF/Print receipt keeps the original invoice reference.
10. CSV export now separates document amount, paid gross, recognized costs and net profit.

No new Render environment variables, Cloudflare Worker changes or Firebase rules are required.

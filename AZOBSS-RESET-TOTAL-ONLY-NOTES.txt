AZOBSS Reset Total Only Fix

Reset button in admin Purchase Records now resets only the user's total purchase counter:
- Unit count shown in summary resets to 0
- Total RM shown in summary resets to RM0
- Purchase history/details are not deleted
- Latest/Last purchase remains visible
- Future purchases after reset will count again from 0

Implementation:
- Stores purchaseTotalResetAtMs and purchaseTotalResetAtClient in Firestore users/{usernameKey}
- Keeps purchaseRecords collection untouched
- Keeps embedded user purchaseRecords untouched

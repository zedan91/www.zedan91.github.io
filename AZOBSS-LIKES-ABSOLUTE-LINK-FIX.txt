AZOBSS Likes Absolute Link Fix

Fix made:
- Likes now saves Software Tools download links as absolute URLs.
- Old relative links on /likes/ are converted at render/click time.
- Example:
  downloads/disable-windows-update-pause-10-years.bat
  becomes
  https://www.azobss.com/Software-Tools/downloads/disable-windows-update-pause-10-years.bat
- This prevents /likes/downloads/... 404 errors.
- CAD and Affiliate relative links are also normalized to their own base paths.

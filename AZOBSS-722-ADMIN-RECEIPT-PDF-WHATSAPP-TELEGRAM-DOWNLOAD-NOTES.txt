AZOBSS Patch 722 - Admin Receipt PDF / WhatsApp / Telegram / Download

Baseline: Patch 721.

Changes:
1. Added a real client-side A4 PDF generator for Sales & Receipts.
2. Customer PDF excludes internal product cost, gateway fee, commission and net profit.
3. Added Download PDF, WhatsApp PDF, Telegram PDF and Print actions.
4. On browsers supporting Web Share with files, the actual PDF is passed to the native share sheet.
5. On unsupported desktop browsers, the PDF is downloaded first and WhatsApp/Telegram is opened with receipt text; attach the downloaded PDF manually.
6. No external PDF library, backend endpoint, Cloudflare Worker or new environment variable is required.

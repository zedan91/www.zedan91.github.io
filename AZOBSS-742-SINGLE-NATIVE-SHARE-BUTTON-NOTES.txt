AZOBSS PATCH 742 - SINGLE NATIVE SHARE BUTTON

- Baseline: Patch 741.
- Replaces separate WhatsApp and Telegram actions with one native Share icon.
- Native Share opens the Windows/phone share panel with the actual PDF file.
- Bulk selection also uses one Share icon for selected PDF files.
- Download, Copy Link, Print, Edit, Delete and Original Invoice Download remain unchanged.
- Telegram availability is controlled by Windows Share targets; Unigram may appear while Telegram Desktop may not register as a share target.

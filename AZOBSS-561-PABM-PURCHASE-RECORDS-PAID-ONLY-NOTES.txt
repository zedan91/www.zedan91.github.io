AZOBSS PATCH 561

- Purchase Records Users now shows only successful / verified PA/BM/SBM/GPS/Syit Piawai/Lot Kadaster purchases.
- Pending, cancelled and failed payment attempts remain stored internally for callback verification and can be checked in Payment Logs.
- Pending-only users no longer create misleading Purchase Records Users cards, totals or Last buy entries.
- Unit and Total are calculated from paid / verified rows only.
- Pending and Reset buttons were removed from the paid purchase summary card.
- No Firebase Rules change is required.
- No Render backend redeploy is required because this patch changes admin display logic only.

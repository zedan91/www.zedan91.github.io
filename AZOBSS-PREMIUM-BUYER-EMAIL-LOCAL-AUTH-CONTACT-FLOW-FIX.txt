AZOBSS Premium Buyer Email Local/Auth/Contact Flow Fix (371)

Purpose:
- Premium Software/CAD checkout and email delivery now use a safer buyer email resolver.

Flow:
1. If email is a real/non-local email, use email.
2. If email is an AZOBSS local alias such as @azobss.local, try authEmail first.
3. If authEmail is missing or still not usable, use contactEmail.
4. .local addresses are kept only as last fallback because they cannot receive customer email.
